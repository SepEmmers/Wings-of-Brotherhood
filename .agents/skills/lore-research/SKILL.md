# Lore Research Skill

Ensures every quest, dragon encounter, telepathic hint, and dialogue line aligns with the original dark-medieval *Wings of the Brotherhood* story (`docs/game story.txt`) and lore (`docs/LORE_COMPENDIUM.md`).

## Steps

1. Read `docs/game story.txt`, `docs/GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md`, and `.agents/rules/story-and-lore-continuity.md`.
2. Check `docs/LORE_COMPENDIUM.md` for the 4 Golden Dragon growth stages, Devildragon blood/heart harvest lore, Brotherhood factions, and city identities.
3. For Book II engineering context, consult `docs/HANDOFF_BOOK2_IMPLEMENTATION.md`.
4. Ensure medieval anachronisms are avoided (no firearms, no modern uniforms, and no *Temeraire* / Napoleonic lore references).
