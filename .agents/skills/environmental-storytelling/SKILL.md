# Environmental Storytelling Skill

Distilled standards for *Wings of the Brotherhood*:

1. **Show, Don't Tell:** Replace prose explanations, solo self-talk, toast pop-ups, and scenery name labels with physical animations, prop clues (ovens, laundry, scaffolds, flour sacks, lanterns), weather/lighting shifts, and sound cues.
2. **Respect Mute Characters:** `Kestrel` (`she/her`) never has text speech captions—communicate via crest flares, wing beats, head nudges, and chirps.
3. **Punchy Zelda Dialogue Cadence:** Keep spoken and telepathic `DialogueBox` lines concise (aim for `<= 2–3` wrapped lines per beat, never `5`-line essays) and keep raw keybinding tags (`[E]`, `[F]`, `[R]`, `[X]`) strictly out of speech text.
4. **Living Medieval World:** Cities and towns need lived-in civic identity (shops, markets, workers, side stories independent of the main conspiracy) rather than sterile quest corridors.
5. **Backlog Sync:** Log unavoidable temporary text/cutscene gaps as `- [ ]` in `docs/ENVIRONMENTAL_STORYTELLING.md` and flip `- [x]` once replaced visually.
