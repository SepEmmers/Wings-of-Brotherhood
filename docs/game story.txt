WINGS OF THE BROTHERHOOD — CANONICAL STORY OUTLINE
(Original Dark-Medieval Dragon Fantasy — Flanders, Brabant & the Meuse Borderlands)

================================================================================
PART 1: WORLD PREMISE & THE SECRET CONSPIRACY
================================================================================

1. THE LIE OF THE CHURCH (THE DEVILDRAGON PLOT)
Publicly, the Church rules medieval towns and trade guilds through fear, preaching that all dragons are soulless monsters to be hunted and purged from Christendom.
Secretly, the Church's inner cabal (led by the High Inquisitor and senior Alchemists) is running a covert continent-spanning harvest:
- Catchpoles, Inquisitors, and hunter guilds use sleeping-draughts, iron net-wagons, and spiked capture rigs to seize dragons ALIVE—draining dragon blood and harvesting dragon hearts.
- Their goal is a forbidden ancient ritual to summon/awaken a monstrous "Devildragon" capable of crushing every kingdom and placing the entire continent under the Church's absolute dominion.

2. THE GOLDEN DRAGON PROPHECY
Ancient carvings older than the Church foretell that only a true GOLDEN DRAGON—uniquely wise, perceptive, anciently powerful, and capable of unifying wild and captive dragonkind—can rally the dragons together and defeat the Devildragon.
- That is why the Church hunted the Black-Scaled Dragon to death in the Whispering Pines, and why Father Anselm and the Inquisitor immediately placed a dawn silver bounty across Bruges on anyone seen carrying a black-with-gold egg.
- The dying Black-Scaled Dragon was the last Shadow-Guardian, camouflaged in soot-black obsidian scales to conceal the Golden Heir egg inside her nest. As the hatchling grows across the campaign, its dark outer shell-plates crack and shed away to reveal true gold underneath.

3. THE PARENTS' BETRAYAL AT OLD HEVERLEE (9 YEARS BEFORE BOOK II)
- The protagonist (named by the player at game start) was born in Louvain.
- When he was 8 years old, his parents—respected veteran Dragon Hunters—were contracted by Church officials to excavate a forgotten dragon-shrine deep in the woods of Old Heverlee.
- Once they uncovered the shrine and realized it held the Devildragon summoning ritual rather than a common beast's lair, they refused to surrender the crucial key relic (and hid it along with clues to its true location).
- To bury the truth forever, Church Inquisitors ambushed and murdered both parents at Old Heverlee, telling Louvain and the surviving children that "a savage dragon killed them on their final hunt." Because of this lie, the boy grew up hating dragons.

4. CLARA'S KIDNAPPING (5 YEARS BEFORE BOOK II — DOUBLE DISCOVERY)
- Left orphaned at 8, the boy and his older sister Clara moved to the Port of Bruges to live with Uncle Willem (a port worker). The boy worked shipping vessels; Clara spun heavy maritime cordage at the Bruges Ropewalk.
- Five years before the main journey (when the boy was 12), Church raiders abducting skilled Bruges rope-makers snatched Clara in the night—leaving behind only a snapped strap of dragon-harness leather tied with her signature Bruges Ropewalk Whipping-Knot.
- At first, the Church seized Clara purely as a Conscripted Rig-Master: her cordage craft was needed at their inland Blood-Harvest Quarry/Fortress to weave reinforced iron-and-hemp harnesses capable of restraining full-grown captive dragons.
- Only LATER did Church records connect Clara to Louvain and realize she is the surviving elder daughter of the Old Heverlee hunters who hid their missing Ritual Key—making her both their forced rig-master and a critical prisoner interrogated over what her parents left behind.

================================================================================
PART 2: PROLOGUE — BOOK I ("SMOKE OVER FLANDERS") [ALREADY IMPLEMENTED]
================================================================================

- Age 12, Port of Bruges: Arriving aboard merchant cog *De Zwaan*, evening harbor errands, visiting Clara at the Ropewalk (Louvain Beeswax & Brass Thimble), supper at Uncle Willem's cottage.
- Dawn Disappearance & Betrayal Attempt: Clara vanishes; finding the snapped dragon-leather strap, the boy blames the secret Brotherhood of Dragon Riders and slips past dawn Church watchmen to snitch—only to be intercepted by the Brotherhood before reaching the Inquisitor.
- Spared by Aunt Anna: Marked for death to keep the Sanctuary hidden, the boy is spared by Captain Anna (secretly his mother's sister, though she conceals this!). Kept under Saddler Hendrick and Sister Maren for five years (Age 12 -> 14 -> 17), he heals a scarred silver-slate courier dragon named Kestrel and learns rope-rigging aboard Anna's emerald dragon *Virelai*.
- Sorties & Obsidian Hollow (Age 17): During a storm recon sortie, an unknown Black-Scaled Dragon ambushes them; cast down into the Whispering Pines, the boy discovers the mortally wounded Black-Scaled Dragon shielding a black-and-gold egg against Church hunters. Carrying the egg across freezing clearings to Watchtower Hearth, he returns to the Bruges Sanctuary.
- Council Debate & Midnight Heist: Saddler Hendrick and the Council elders demand the egg be locked under iron vault in Ghent. Sent to scout Bruges City, the boy tails Father Anselm to Bruges Keep and overhears the Inquisitor ordering a dawn bounty on any black-with-gold egg. Returning to Sanctuary, the boy lies to Anna and Hendrick, steals the egg back at midnight, and escapes into the night sky atop Kestrel as the main story begins.

================================================================================
PART 3: MAIN STORY — BOOK II+ ("THE GOLDEN HEIR") [3-ACT DUAL-PERSPECTIVE CAMPAIGN]
================================================================================

STRUCTURAL PILLAR — DUAL PERSPECTIVES & KESTREL COURIER LINK:
- After escaping Bruges, the story alternates between TWO playable perspectives:
  1. THE BOY'S PERSPECTIVE (Ground Journey, Cities, Hatchling Bond & Shrines):
     Travels across Flanders, Brabant, and the Meuse highlands with the hatching Golden Dragon following on foot (tucked into a hooded cloak-sling inside walled cities). Uncovers his parents' mystery, hunts clues to Clara, solves Golden Shrines, and raises the dragon through 4 gameplay-changing growth stages.
  2. ANNA'S PERSPECTIVE (Brotherhood Politics, Virelai Aerial Raids & Dragon Rescues):
     Back at Sanctuary and across regional skies aboard *Virelai*, Anna stalls Saddler Hendrick and the cautious Council elders from hunting her nephew, sends scouts and supply drops ahead to aid the Boy's route, intercepts Church blood-harvest barges/convoys, and rescues captive wild dragons—building the dragon coalition needed for the finale.
- KESTREL'S ROLE:
  Once Kestrel sets the Boy down safely in Brabant outside walled Louvain, she cannot hover openly near bounty-lit cities. Kestrel remains with Anna's forward scouts as the **main winged messenger between Anna and the Boy**—carrying dispatches, intel, and supplies back and forth and linking their alternating chapters.

DRAGON GROWTH & TELEPATHIC BOND (4 STAGES):
- Stage 1 (Hatchling — Act I): Cat-sized soot-black dragonet with flickering gold scale-seams. Mute chirps/purrs at first, sending faint wordless feelings before unlocking early Navi-style telepathic nudges directly into the Boy's mind. Gameplay: vent/grate crawling, ember puffs for torches/briars, gold-sense chirps near prophetic clues.
- Stage 2 (Fledgling — Act II): Wolf-sized; dark plates shed along wings and crest revealing radiant gold. Clear telepathic voice (heard only by the Boy) offering tactical hints, observations, and dry curiosity. Gameplay: short glide-boosts across chasms, knot-snare combo lunges against armored Catchpoles, sonic resonance cracks on weak masonry/alarm bells.
- Stage 3 (Young Drake — Early Act III): Pony-sized armored gold-and-obsidian silhouette; too large to conceal in towns. Gameplay: fortress wall hops, melting Golden Flame against iron portcullises/shields, wing-shield protection against crossbow volleys.
- Stage 4 (Awakened Golden Dragon — Climax): Full sovereign golden form. Unlocks full aerial flight and the Golden Rally—awakening and uniting wild and Brotherhood dragons against the Church's Devildragon!

ACT I: FLEMISH FENS -> LOUVAIN -> OLD HEVERLEE
- Boy — Leg 1 (Brabant Fens & First Camp): Kestrel lands in misty river ruins west of Louvain before returning to Anna's scout roost. At the first sheltered campfire, the obsidian-and-gold egg hatches! The tiny dragonet imprints on the Boy and learns to crawl narrow vents and puff sparks.
- Boy — Hub 1 (Louvain — City of Bells & Locked Archives):
  - Character: Scholarly walled river city on the Dijle with stone bell-foundries, guild cloisters, dyers' alleys, and an old Hunters' Tavern—now patrolled by Catchpoles posting dawn bounty notices.
  - Plot: Concealing the hatchling inside his hooded travel-sling, the Boy visits his childhood parish and the Hunters' Guild Tavern, learning his parents burned their Church contracts right before their final trip. Infiltrating the Abbey Scriptorium (using the hatchling to slip through crypt vents and light dark vaults), he uncovers two truths: (1) his parents' deaths were signed off by Church Decree over a missing "Old Heverlee Shrine Key," and (2) a 5-year-old requisition warrant transferred *"Clara of Bruges, rope-maker"* east to the Church's **Namur Iron Quarry & Fortress**. Bounty hounds ambush the courtyard; the cornered hatchling emits a blinding Golden Resonance shriek that shatters stained glass, letting the Boy flee south toward Old Heverlee.
  - Side Stories: (a) Deaf Bell-Founder needing river-clay & a crane knot-repair (rewards Bronze Resonance Chime); (b) Dyers' Quarter Herbalist brewing Smoke-Pellets/Sleep-Salts; (c) One-Eyed Old Heverlee Survivor confessing Inquisitors ambushed his comrades in the trees once his pawned compass/ring is recovered.
- Boy — Leg 2 (Old Heverlee Woods & First Golden Shrine):
  - Playable haunted forest & ruined shrine site where the parents died. With the hatchling's Golden Sense (and Louvain clues), the Boy discovers the hidden shrine chamber—learning what the Devildragon ritual actually requires (blood and hearts of dragons!) and recovering the missing **Old Heverlee Relic Key** his parents died hiding!
- Anna — Parallel Chapter 1 (Sanctuary Standoff & First Blood-Barge Raid):
  - Kestrel reaches Anna with the Boy's Louvain/Old Heverlee dispatch. Anna deceives Hendrick's search party (pointing them toward the coast while sending scouts ahead to Hesbaye with supplies for her nephew), then flies *Virelai* to smash a Church river-chain convoy hauling sedated captive dragons—discovering blood-vial harvest crates firsthand.

ACT II: HESBAYE UPLANDS -> NAMUR CLIFF-CITADEL
- Boy — Leg 3 (Hesbaye Uplands Route):
  - Long wilderness trek east along broken Roman roads, hunter roadblocks, and wind-scoured hills. Using Anna's Kestrel-dropped supplies and feeding/bonding at wild campfires (unlocking the Hatchling -> Stage 2 **Fledgling** shed!), the duo explores a buried Dragon Waystation shrine and overcomes heavy patrols using glide-boosts and knot-combo combat.
- Boy — Hub 2 (Namur Cliff-Citadel — The Iron & Quarry City):
  - Character: Sheer limestone cliff-fortress overlooking the Meuse River—smoky iron foundries, river barge locks, cliffside cable hoists, and heavily guarded Church dragon-rigging workshops.
  - Plot: Working with river smugglers and Anna's hidden city contacts, the Boy infiltrates the Quarry Rig-Works—finally seeing **Clara** alive behind forge bars, forced to weave spiked suppression harnesses while Inquisitors press her about Old Heverlee! Before he can cut her free, the High Inquisitor arrives, recognizes Clara's tie to the stolen Golden Child, and moves Clara plus the harvested dragon hearts east by armored river-barge toward the **Blood-Citadel** to begin the Devildragon summoning.
  - Side Stories: Quarry Forge-Saboteurs freeing captive miners; Meuse Ferryman smuggling dragon scales; Broken Bellows Master upgrading the Boy's grapple/gear.
- Anna — Parallel Chapter 2 (Council Rift & Mass Rescue):
  - With the Sanctuary Council threatening open arrest, Anna breaks openly with Hendrick—taking Matthijs's loyalists, Kestrel, and *Virelai* on a daring multi-dragon raid to liberate a Church holding-pen, rallying dozens of freed dragons behind her as she flies east to reinforce the Boy.

ACT III: ARDENNES HIGHLANDS -> THE CHURCH BLOOD-CITADEL (CONVERGENCE)
- Boy — Leg 4 (Ardennes Blackwood & Sunken Dragon-Cairn):
  - Harsh granite crags and ancient forest tombs where the Fledgling grows into a Stage 3 **Young Drake** (golden armor plates, wall-vaults, Golden Flame). Using the Old Heverlee Relic Key at the Sunken Cairn, the Boy unlocks the full prophecy—and learns the truth from Anna's forward scouts / Kestrel as the armies converge.
- Finale — The Blood-Citadel & Awakening of the Golden Dragon:
  - As the High Inquisitor initiates the blood-and-heart ritual inside the Citadel chasm—and the terrifying **Devildragon** begins tearing out of the summoning pit—**Anna and *Virelai* swoop down from the storm clouds** to rescue the Boy, finally revealing she is his mother's sister!
  - Together with **Clara** (who turns her forced Ropewalk harness lines into sabotage anchors to snap the Devildragon's ritual chains) and **Kestrel**, the Boy's bond triggers the final transformation: **Stage 4 — The Awakened Golden Dragon**.
  - Unleashing a resonant Golden Roar across the sky, the Golden Dragon rallies *Virelai*, Kestrel, and every freed wild dragon into a coordinated sky-and-citadel battle—destroying the Devildragon, shattering the Church's cabal, reuniting the Boy with Clara and Aunt Anna, and ushering in a new dawn where dragons walk and fly free.
