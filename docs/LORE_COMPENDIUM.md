# Wings of the Brotherhood — Lore Compendium & World Reference

> **Purpose:** Authoritative original worldbuilding, dragon taxonomy, Church conspiracy lore, Brotherhood organization, and Zelda-style companion/gameplay reference for *Wings of the Brotherhood* — an original dark-medieval 2D Top-Down Action-Adventure RPG set across Flanders, Brabant, and the Meuse highlands.

---

## 1. Dragon Lineages & World Fauna

In this medieval world, wild dragons range from animal-cunning flyers to rare, anciently intelligent elders (*like Virelai*). True speech among dragons is rare and mistrusted by human townsfolk, making **silent or telepathic communication** vital to survival under Church rule.

### 1.1 The Mythic Golden Dragon Bloodline (The Prophecy Lineage)
- **Mythic Status:** Revered in pre-Church stone carvings as sovereigns of wisdom, memory, and sky. Ancient prophecy foretells that **only a Golden Dragon** possesses the intellect, commanding voice, and radiant power to **rally all fractured dragon breeds into a single army** capable of defeating the Church's **Devildragon**.
- **The Shadow-Guardian Camouflage:** To protect the last Golden Egg from Church purges, its guardian/mother—the **Black-Scaled Dragon** encountered in Obsidian Hollow—wore dull soot-obsidian scales over golden bloodlines. Her egg appeared pitch-black threaded with faint molten gold veins.
- **4-Stage Growth & Shedding:** As the hatchling matures through care, nourishment, and ancient Golden Shrines, its dark outer plates flake away stage by stage to reveal true metallic gold scales beneath:

### 1.3 The Devildragon Abomination (The Church's Secret Goal)
- **What It Is:** Not a natural dragon breed, but an ancient, tyrannical nightmare-entity sealed beneath forgotten blood-altars.
- **How It Is Summoned:** Requires massive quantities of **living dragon blood and harvested dragon hearts** distilled by Church alchemists, bound inside reinforced iron-and-hemp siege rigging, and awakened at the **Blood-Citadel chasm**.
- **Strategic Consequence:** Explains *why* the Church pays gold for **live dragon captures** (sleeping-draughts, net-wagons, iron cages) and abducts master rope-makers like **Clara**—every captive dragon feeds the ritual.

## 2. Medieval Factions & Institutions

### 2.1 The Church & Inquisitor Cabal
- **Public Doctrine:** Preaches from every pulpit (St. Donatian's in Bruges, Abbey spires in Louvain) that dragons are unholy serpents menacing townsfolk, rallying Catchpoles, Arbalesters, and mercenary Dragon Hunters under pious crusades.
- **Inner Cabal Secret:** Senior Inquisitors and Alchemists stockpile dragon hearts and blood inside fortified quarries (**Namur**) and highlands (**Blood-Citadel**). Anyone uncovering the excavation at **Old Heverlee** or carrying a black-and-gold egg faces immediate silent execution or town-wide silver bounties.
- **Field Gear:** Heavy crossbows (*arbalests*), oak-and-iron tower shields, sleeping-draught wagons, forged wing-cuffs, and **Acoustic Ward-Bells** designed to disorient flying dragons.

### 2.2 The Secret Brotherhood of Dragon Riders
- **Origin & Purpose:** Outlawed network of riders, saddlers, herbalists, and scouts fighting to save wild dragons from Church slaughter and capture.
- **Internal Rift (Book II+):**
  - **The Cautious Council (Saddler Hendrick & Ghent Elders):** Fear total extermination if the Church discovers a prophetic egg; demand secrecy, iron vaults, and hunting down rogue members who defy Council orders.
  - **Anna's Reformer Cell (Captain Anna, *Virelai*, Bellman Matthijs & Forward Scouts):** Secretly stalls the Council while dispatching scouts, supply drops, and **Kestrel** ahead to aid the Boy's quest—recognizing that hiding in cliff caves won't stop the Church's continental blood-harvest.

### 2.3 Medieval Harness & Ropewalk Craft
- **No Modern Gear:** All aerial and ground gear reflects genuine medieval craft: tarred hemp cordage, beeswaxed threads, iron D-rings, hand-forged carabiners, leather breast-straps, and oak-board knots (*Clove Hitch*, *Bruges Whipping-Knot*, *Bowline*, *Brotherhood Harness Splice*).
- **Clara's Mastery:** Trained at the **Bruges Ropewalk**, Clara weaves ultra-high-tension cordage. Her skill is why Church raiders abducted her five years ago to build captive-dragon suppression harnesses at Namur—before discovering she is also the daughter of the hunters who hid the **Old Heverlee Ritual Key**.

## 3. Geography of the Campaign (Flanders → Brabant → Meuse → Ardennes)

| Region / Hub | Narrative & Atmospheric Identity | Key Landmarks & Gameplay Flavor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Port & Walled City of Bruges** *(Prologue)* | Bustling Hanseatic canal-port, Ropewalk, Uncle Willem's hearth, Grote Markt, St. Donatian's Church, and Brotherhood Sanctuary cliffs. | Dock errands, canal barge hopping, dawn watchman stealth, priest tailing, midnight Sanctuary heist. |
| **Brabant Fens** *(Act I Leg 1)* | Misty peat marshes, sunken stone mills, flooded causeways west of Louvain. | First landing with **Kestrel**; **Egg Hatching** at sheltered ruins; teaches Hatchling vent-crawl & ember-puff. |
| **Louvain** *(Act I Hub 1)* | Scholarly walled river city along the Dijle: steep bell-foundries, Abbey Scriptorium, dyers' quarter, and the old Hunters' Tavern. | Conceal hatchling in hooded cloak-sling; Wanted Posters; Scriptorium infiltration; 3 city side stories; Golden Resonance breakout. |
| **Old Heverlee Woods** *(Act I Leg 2)* | Haunted beech & pine woodlands outside Louvain where the Boy's parents were killed 9 years ago. | Uncover parents' ambush ruins; solve the **First Golden Dragon Shrine**; claim the **Old Heverlee Relic Key**; learn the truth of the blood-harvest. |
| **Hesbaye Uplands** *(Act II Leg 3)* | Wind-scoured chalk ridges, shattered Roman roads, Church roadblocks, and hidden Anna scout caches. | Long overland trek; campfire feeding & **Stage 2 Fledgling** growth; glide-boost chasm puzzles & knot-combo patrols. |
| **Namur Cliff-Citadel** *(Act II Hub 2)* | Gritty iron-and-limestone fortress towering over the Meuse River: quarry hoists, smelting foundries, barge locks, and dragon rig-works. | River-smuggler alliances; infiltrating the Forge & Rig-Works; discovering **Clara** alive before the High Inquisitor moves the harvest east. |
| **Ardennes Highlands & Blood-Citadel** *(Act III Finale)* | Craggy slate peaks, Sunken Dragon-Cairn, and the Church's fortified ritual chasm. | Stage 3 **Young Drake** wall-vaults & Golden Flame; dual-perspective convergence with **Anna, *Virelai*, Kestrel, Clara**, and **Stage 4 Awakened Golden Dragon** finale. |
